# Vaccines and flu shots

## Flu shots

Seasonal flu shots are available from September through March for everyone 6 months and older.
Adults can get a flu shot without an appointment at the front desk on weekdays from 8:00 AM to
5:30 PM (not between 12:00 and 1:00 PM). Children's flu shots are given by appointment with
pediatrics, or during a scheduled visit. You can also ask for a flu shot at any appointment.

## COVID-19 and other adult vaccines

We offer updated COVID-19 vaccines, Tdap (tetanus, diphtheria and whooping cough), shingles,
pneumonia, hepatitis B and HPV vaccines. These are given by appointment; book a visit with one
of our family medicine providers.

## Childhood vaccines

Routine childhood and school vaccines are given by Dr. Sofia Marquez in pediatrics, usually as
part of a well-child visit. Bring your child's vaccination record. We can fill in school and camp
immunization forms.

## Travel vaccines

We do not offer travel vaccines such as yellow fever or typhoid. We can refer you to a travel
health clinic.

## Cost

Most insurance plans, including Medicare Part B for flu and pneumonia shots, cover recommended
vaccines at no cost to you. Bring your insurance card.
