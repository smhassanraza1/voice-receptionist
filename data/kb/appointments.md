# Booking appointments

## How to book

You can book, reschedule or cancel an appointment by phone with our front desk or our automated
phone assistant, or online through the patient portal.

## How far ahead you can book

Appointments can be booked up to **28 days** in advance. Same-day appointments can be booked up
to one hour before the start time, when there are openings.

## Appointment length

Standard appointments are 30 minutes long. If you need more time, for example to discuss several
concerns, tell us when booking and we can reserve two back-to-back times.

## Your confirmation code

Every appointment has a confirmation code that looks like LKC-7Q2M. Keep it handy: together with
your date of birth, it lets you check, change or cancel your appointment by phone.

## Reminders

We send a reminder by text message two days before your appointment, with a link to confirm,
reschedule or cancel.

## Arriving for your visit

Please arrive 10 minutes before your appointment to check in, or 20 minutes early if you are a
new patient and have not filled out your forms online. If you arrive more than 15 minutes late we
may need to reschedule.

## Choosing a provider

You can ask for a specific provider, a specialty, a day, or a morning or afternoon time. If you
would like a provider who speaks your language, let us know.
