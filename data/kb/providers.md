# Our providers and specialties

## Family medicine

**Dr. Priya Raman** is a family medicine physician who sees patients of all ages from 2 years
up, Monday through Friday. She speaks English and Hindi and is accepting new patients.

**Dr. Kenji Watanabe** is a family medicine physician who sees patients on Tuesdays, Wednesdays,
Fridays and Saturday mornings. He speaks English and Japanese and is accepting new patients.

Family medicine covers checkups and physicals, chronic conditions such as diabetes and high blood
pressure, sick visits, vaccines, and routine women's health care.

## Cardiology

**Dr. Daniel Okafor** is our cardiologist, seeing patients with heart conditions, high blood
pressure, chest pain follow-ups and heart rhythm problems. He sees patients on Tuesdays and
Thursdays, speaks English, and is accepting new patients. Some insurance plans require a referral.

## Pediatrics

**Dr. Sofia Marquez** is our pediatrician, caring for newborns, children and teens up to age 18.
She sees patients on Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays and Saturday mornings, speaks English and
Spanish, and is accepting new patients.

## Dermatology

**Dr. Hannah Weiss** is our dermatologist, treating skin, hair and nail conditions such as acne,
eczema, psoriasis and rashes, and doing skin cancer checks. She sees patients on Mondays and
Thursdays, speaks English and German, and is accepting new patients.

## Women's health

**Alex Moreau, NP** is a nurse practitioner in women's health, offering gynecological exams, Pap
tests, contraception, and menopause care. Alex sees patients on Mondays, Tuesdays and Fridays
and speaks English and French. Alex is currently seeing established patients only and is not
accepting new patients; new patients can book routine women's health care with our family
medicine providers.

## Services we don't offer

We do not offer surgery, obstetrics (prenatal care and delivery), physical therapy or travel
medicine. We are happy to refer you.
