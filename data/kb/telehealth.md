# Telehealth video visits

## What telehealth is good for

A telehealth visit is a video appointment with your provider from your phone, tablet or
computer. Video visits work well for:

- Follow-up visits and medication checks
- Reviewing test results
- Minor illnesses such as colds, rashes or urinary symptoms
- Mental health check-ins

## When you need to come in

Physical exams, vaccines, lab work, procedures and anything that needs a hands-on examination
must be done in person. Never use telehealth for an emergency; call 911 instead.

## How to book a video visit

Video visits use the same appointment times as in-person visits. When you book, say that you
would like a telehealth visit and include it in the reason for the visit, for example
"telehealth follow-up". Dr. Hannah Weiss (dermatology) can review many skin concerns by video if
you can share clear photos.

## Joining the visit

About 15 minutes before your appointment we will text and email you a secure link. You do not
need to install an app. Find a quiet, private, well-lit place, and make sure you are physically
located in the state during the visit.

## Cost

Telehealth visits are billed like office visits. Your usual copay applies, and all of the
insurance plans we accept cover video visits.
