# Cancellation, rescheduling and no-show policy

## How to cancel or reschedule

You can cancel or reschedule by phone at any time, including with our automated assistant, or
through the patient portal. To protect your privacy we will ask for your date of birth plus your
confirmation code or the phone number on file.

## Late cancellation fee

Please give us at least 24 hours' notice. Appointments cancelled less than 24 hours before the
start time are late cancellations and are charged a **$50 late cancellation fee**. Cancelling 24
hours or more in advance is free.

## Rescheduling

Rescheduling to another available time is free of charge. You keep the same confirmation code
when you reschedule.

## No-shows

If you miss an appointment without cancelling, it is recorded as a no-show and the same $50 fee
applies. After three no-shows within 12 months, we may ask you to book only same-day
appointments.

## Running late

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, we may need to reschedule your visit so that other
patients are seen on time.

## Exceptions

We understand emergencies happen. If you had to miss an appointment because of an emergency or
severe weather, contact our billing team and we will review the fee. Insurance does not cover
cancellation or no-show fees.
