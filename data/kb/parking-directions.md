# Parking and directions

## Parking

Free parking is available in the Harbor Medical Building garage, directly behind the building.
The garage entrance is on Pier Lane. Bring your parking ticket to the front desk and we will
validate it for up to three hours.

Four accessible parking spaces are on the ground level of the garage, next to the elevator.
There is a 15-minute drop-off zone in front of the main entrance on Harbor Street for patients
who need help getting to the door.

## Public transit

Bus routes 4 and 12 stop at Harbor Street and Main Street, one block from the clinic. The
Lakeside ferry terminal is a ten-minute walk to the east along the waterfront.

## Driving directions

From the highway, take the Lakeside / Harbor exit and follow Main Street toward the waterfront.
Turn left onto Harbor Street; the Harbor Medical Building is on the right after the second
traffic light. Turn right onto Pier Lane for the garage.

## Bicycles and rideshare

A covered bike rack is next to the main entrance. Rideshare and taxi drivers can use the drop-off
zone on Harbor Street.

## Finding the clinic inside the building

Take the elevator or the stairs to the second floor. Lakeside Family Clinic is Suite 200, the
first door on the left. Check in at the front desk when you arrive.
