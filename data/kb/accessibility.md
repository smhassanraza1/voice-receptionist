# Accessibility

## Getting into the building

The Harbor Medical Building has a step-free main entrance with automatic doors on Harbor Street.
An elevator goes to the second floor, where the clinic is (Suite 200). Four accessible parking
spaces are on the ground level of the garage next to the elevator, and there is a drop-off zone
at the front entrance.

## Inside the clinic

- Wide doorways and hallways for wheelchairs and walkers
- Accessible restrooms
- Exam rooms with height-adjustable exam tables
- A wheelchair you can borrow at the front desk

## Service animals

Trained service animals, such as guide dogs, are welcome everywhere in the clinic. Pets and
emotional support animals are not allowed inside.

## Hearing and vision

- ASL interpreters, in person with 48 hours' notice or by video
- Relay calls are welcome; dial 711 to reach us through relay services
- Large-print forms, and staff who can read forms aloud with you

## Extra time and support

If you need a longer appointment, a quiet waiting area, or help completing forms, tell us when you
book and we will plan for it. A support person or caregiver is welcome to come with you.
