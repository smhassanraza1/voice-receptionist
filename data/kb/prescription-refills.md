# Prescription refills

## How to request a refill

The fastest way to refill a prescription is to ask your pharmacy to send us a refill request.
You can also request a refill through the patient portal, or call the front desk during clinic
hours and we will pass the request to your provider.

## How long refills take

Please allow **two business days** for refill requests to be reviewed. Requests received on a
Friday afternoon or a weekend are handled the next business day. Try to request refills a week
before you run out.

## When a visit is needed

To keep refilling a long-term medication, you need to have been seen by one of our providers in
the past 12 months. Some medicines, such as blood pressure or diabetes medication, may need a
recent lab test or follow-up visit before they can be refilled.

## Controlled substances

Controlled medications (for example, some pain, sleep or ADHD medicines) cannot be refilled by
phone. They require a visit or a scheduled follow-up with your provider.

## Antibiotics and new prescriptions

We cannot prescribe antibiotics or new medications without an appointment. A telehealth video
visit is often a quick option.

## If you have run out

If you have run out of an important medication, tell us when you call so the request is marked
urgent. After hours, call our main number and press 2 for the nurse line.
