# Lab work and fasting preparation

## On-site lab

Our lab draws blood and collects samples Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM, closed from
12:00 to 1:00 PM. There is no lab on Saturdays. You do not need an appointment for lab work, but
you do need a lab order from your provider.

## Fasting before blood tests

Some blood tests, such as a fasting cholesterol (lipid) panel or a fasting blood glucose test,
require you to fast. Your lab order will say if fasting is needed.

- Do not eat or drink anything except water for **8 to 12 hours** before your test.
- Drinking plain water is fine and helps make the blood draw easier.
- Avoid coffee, tea, juice, chewing gum and alcohol while fasting.
- Take your usual medications with water unless your provider told you otherwise.
- If you have diabetes and take insulin or other glucose-lowering medicine, ask your provider
  how to adjust it before fasting.

Morning appointments make fasting easier, because most of the fasting happens overnight.

## Urine and other samples

For urine tests, we will give you a sterile container and instructions at the lab. Some tests
need a first-morning sample; your provider will tell you if so.

## What to bring

Bring your photo ID, insurance card and lab order. Wear a short-sleeved shirt or one with sleeves
that roll up easily.
