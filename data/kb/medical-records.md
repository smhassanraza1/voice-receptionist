# Medical records and privacy

## Your privacy

We protect your health information under federal privacy law (HIPAA). We only use and share it
to treat you, to bill for your care, and to run our clinic, or when you give written permission.
Our Notice of Privacy Practices is available at the front desk and in the patient portal.

## Verifying your identity

Before we discuss or change anything about your care or appointments, we verify your identity.
By phone, we ask for your date of birth plus your appointment confirmation code or the phone
number on file. For your protection, we will not say whether someone is a patient here unless
their identity is verified.

## Who can see your information

We do not share your information with family members, employers or anyone else unless you have
authorized them in writing, or they are your legal representative. Parents and guardians can
access the records of children under 18.

## Requesting a copy of your records

Request your records through the patient portal or with a records request form at the front
desk. Electronic copies are free. We fulfill requests within 30 days, and usually much sooner.
Paper copies of large records may have a small copying fee.

## Sending records to another provider

To send your records to another doctor or hospital, sign a release form listing who should
receive them. We can also request records from your previous providers.

## Correcting your records

If you believe something in your record is wrong, you can ask us in writing to correct it.
