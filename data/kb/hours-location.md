# Clinic hours and location

## Address

Lakeside Family Clinic is at **120 Harbor Street, Suite 200, Lakeside**. We are on the second
floor of the Harbor Medical Building, above the pharmacy. Our main phone number is
(555) 010-0000.

## Opening hours

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday to Friday | 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM |
| Saturday | 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM |
| Sunday | Closed |

On weekdays our providers do not see patients between 12:00 and 1:00 PM (lunch), but the phone
lines and front desk stay open. Saturday mornings have no lunch break.

## Holidays

The clinic is closed on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day,
Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. On the day before Thanksgiving and on Christmas Eve we close
at 1:00 PM. When we are closed, the after-hours nurse line is available by calling our main
number and pressing 2.

## Which provider works when

Not every provider is in every day. For example, cardiology (Dr. Daniel Okafor) is only on
Tuesdays and Thursdays, and Saturday appointments are with Dr. Sofia Marquez (pediatrics) or
Dr. Kenji Watanabe (family medicine). See "Our providers and specialties" for each provider's
schedule.
