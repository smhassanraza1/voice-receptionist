# Pediatric care for children and teens

## Our pediatrician

Dr. Sofia Marquez cares for children from newborns through age 18. She sees patients on
Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays and Saturday mornings, and speaks English and Spanish. She is
accepting new patients.

Our family medicine providers, Dr. Priya Raman and Dr. Kenji Watanabe, also see children aged 2
and older.

## Visits we offer

- Newborn visits within the first week after leaving the hospital
- Well-child checkups and developmental screening
- Childhood and school vaccines
- Sick visits for fevers, ear infections, coughs and rashes
- Sports, school and camp physicals and forms
- Teen health visits

## Same-day sick visits

We keep some same-day appointments for sick children. Call in the morning for the best choice of
times. If your child has trouble breathing, is unusually drowsy or hard to wake, or a baby under
3 months has a fever of 100.4°F (38°C) or higher, call 911 or go to the emergency room.

## Parents and guardians

A parent or legal guardian must come to the first visit and give consent for treatment of
patients under 18. Another adult can bring the child to later visits with a signed consent form.

## School forms

Allow three business days for us to complete school, camp or sports forms if your child does not
need a visit.
