# Interpreters and languages we speak

## Free interpreter services

Interpreter services are free for every patient. We provide professional medical interpreters by
phone or video in more than 200 languages, at any appointment and when you call. Just tell us
which language you prefer.

## Providers who speak other languages

All of our providers speak English. Several also speak another language:

- Spanish: Dr. Sofia Marquez (pediatrics)
- Hindi: Dr. Priya Raman (family medicine)
- Japanese: Dr. Kenji Watanabe (family medicine)
- German: Dr. Hannah Weiss (dermatology)
- French: Alex Moreau, NP (women's health)

You can ask to book with a provider who speaks your language.

## Sign language

American Sign Language (ASL) interpreters can attend your visit in person. Please ask at least
48 hours before your appointment. Video ASL interpreting is available on shorter notice.

## Family members as interpreters

For accuracy and privacy, we use professional interpreters rather than family members or friends,
and we never ask children to interpret.

## Translated documents

Registration forms and visit instructions are available in Spanish. We can have other documents
translated on request.
