# Getting your test results

## How long results take

- Most routine blood tests: 1 to 3 business days
- Imaging reports (X-ray, ultrasound): 2 to 5 business days
- Biopsies and pathology: 7 to 10 business days
- Tests sent to outside labs can take longer

## How you receive results

Results are posted to the patient portal as soon as they are reviewed by your provider. You will
get a text or email when a new result is available. If you do not use the portal, we will mail
your results or call you.

## Abnormal results

If a result needs follow-up, your provider or a nurse will call you to explain it and talk about
next steps. No news does not always mean good news, so if you have not heard from us within the
expected time, please call.

## Questions about your results

Front desk staff and our phone assistant cannot interpret test results. If you have questions,
we can send a message to your provider's nurse, who will call you back, usually within one
business day. You can also book a follow-up appointment or a telehealth visit to go over results.

## Privacy

For your privacy, we only discuss results with you or with someone you have authorized in
writing.
