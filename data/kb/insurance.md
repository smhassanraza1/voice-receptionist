# Accepted insurance plans

## Plans we accept

Lakeside Family Clinic is in network with these insurance plans:

- BlueHarbor PPO
- Northwind HMO
- Medicare Part B
- Evergreen Health Choice
- Summit Mutual EPO

These are the only plans we are currently in network with. We do not currently accept Medicaid.
If your plan is not on this list, you can still be seen as a self-pay patient, or you can check
with your insurer about out-of-network benefits.

## HMO referrals

Northwind HMO members usually need a referral from their primary care provider before seeing a
specialist such as cardiology or dermatology. If one of our family medicine providers is your
primary care provider, we can arrange the referral for you.

## Medicare

We accept Medicare Part B for office visits, preventive care, lab work and vaccines covered by
Medicare. Please bring your red, white and blue Medicare card and any supplemental plan card.

## Self-pay

Patients without insurance pay our self-pay rates at the time of the visit. A standard office
visit is $140 for self-pay patients, and a new patient visit is $190. Ask about a payment plan if
you need one.

## Bring your card

Please bring your current insurance card to every visit, and tell the front desk if your
coverage has changed. Questions about a specific claim or bill are handled by our billing team.
