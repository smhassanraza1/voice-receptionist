# New patient intake and what to bring

## Becoming a patient

We welcome new patients of all ages. Most of our providers are accepting new patients; the
exception is Alex Moreau, NP (women's health), who is currently seeing established patients
only. New patients can book with any other provider, including our family medicine providers,
who also offer routine women's health care.

## Before your first visit

Complete the new patient registration forms online through the patient portal before your
visit, or arrive 20 minutes early to fill them out at the front desk. The forms ask about your
medical history, current medications, allergies and emergency contact.

## What to bring

- A photo ID, such as a driver's license or passport
- Your insurance card (and Medicare card, if you have one)
- A list of your current medications, including doses, vitamins and supplements
- Records or recent test results from your previous doctor, if you have them
- A referral, if your insurance plan requires one (for example, Northwind HMO for specialists)
- Your glasses or hearing aids, if you use them

## Children and teens

Patients under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian at their first visit. Please
bring the child's vaccination record.

## Transferring records

If you would like your previous doctor to send your records, sign a records release form at the
front desk and we will request them for you.
