# Billing and payment options

## Paying your bill

You can pay a balance:

- Online through the patient portal
- With a secure payment link that we text or email to you
- In person at the front desk with cash, check, or a credit or debit card

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, and HSA or FSA cards.

## We never take card numbers over the phone

For your security, our staff and our phone assistant will **never ask you to read out a credit
card number over the phone**. If you want to pay while you are on a call, we will send you a
secure payment link by text or email instead.

## Payment plans

If your balance is over $200, you can set up an interest-free payment plan and pay in monthly
installments over up to 12 months. There is no fee to set up a plan. Ask our billing team to
arrange it.

## Statements and copays

Copays are collected at check-in. After your insurance processes a claim, we send a statement for
any remaining balance. Payment is due within 30 days of the statement date.

## Billing questions

Our billing team can answer questions about your balance, an invoice, an insurance claim, or a
payment plan. For account-specific questions we first verify your identity.

## Financial assistance

Patients with limited income may qualify for a discount through our financial assistance
program. Ask the billing team for an application.
