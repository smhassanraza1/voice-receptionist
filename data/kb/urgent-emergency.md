# Urgent care, emergencies and the after-hours nurse line

## Call 911 for emergencies

Call **911** or go to the nearest emergency room right away for:

- Chest pain or pressure, or signs of a heart attack
- Trouble breathing or choking
- Signs of a stroke: face drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech
- Severe bleeding, a serious injury, or a head injury with confusion
- Fainting, seizures, or sudden severe pain
- A severe allergic reaction

Do not wait for a clinic appointment for an emergency.

## Mental health crisis

If you or someone else is thinking about suicide or is in emotional crisis, call or text **988**,
the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, available 24 hours a day. If there is immediate danger, call 911.

## Poisoning

For a possible poisoning, call Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222.

## Urgent but not an emergency

For problems that need care soon but are not life-threatening, such as a fever, sore throat,
ear pain, a minor cut, or a urinary infection, call us. We keep same-day appointments every day
we are open.

## After-hours nurse line

When the clinic is closed, call our main number, (555) 010-0000, and press 2 to speak with a
registered nurse. The nurse line is available evenings, overnight, weekends and holidays. The
nurse can advise you on whether to go to the emergency room, wait for a clinic appointment, or
care for yourself at home.
